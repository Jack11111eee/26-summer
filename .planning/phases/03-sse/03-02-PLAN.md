## SSE 事件契约（sse.js 1-13 注释逐字段对齐——RESEARCH 92 行全文核对）
帧格式：`data: {json.dumps(payload, ensure_ascii=False)}\n\n`（每帧双换行；中文 reply 保样）
事件序列（三段）：
- decision：{"type":"decision","action":<回落后 action>,"reason":decision.reason,"score_live":decision.score_live,"answer_state":decision.answer_state,"evidence_sufficient":decision.evidence_sufficient,"reply":decision.reply}——sse.js onDecision 消费 action/reason/score_live；reply 放入 decision 帧（sse.js 形态 B 对齐 + 幂等快照含全量——sse.js 不读 decision.reply 无影响，属快照完整性裁量）
- reply×N：{"type":"reply","content":"<chunk>"}——mock 模式按字符等分 4 块（Claude's Discretion——离线可测节奏；分块只影响传输不改语义）；真实模式决策回复整文单块（Anti-pattern 6：不新增 LLM 流式调用面——reply 已在 decision dict）
- done：{"type":"done","action":<action>,"next_question_id":<question_id or None>}——finish/form 时 next_question_id=None；sse.js onDone 消费 next_question_id + action
